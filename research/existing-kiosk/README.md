# Existing ticket kiosk (reference)

Photos of the current Gujarat Metro ticket vending kiosk at Dholakuva Circle station
(Line 2), taken on 30 Oct 2026. They show today's purchase flow for comparison
with this prototype.

| # | Screen | What it shows |
|---|--------|---------------|
| 1 | [Welcome](01-welcome.jpg) | "Purchase QR Ticket", language buttons (Hindi, Gujarati), QR/UPI only |
| 2 | [Destination list, page 1](02-destination-list-page-1.jpg) | Station names as a grid of text buttons |
| 3 | [Destination list, page 2](03-destination-list-page-2.jpg) | More station buttons, paged with arrows |
| 4 | [Destination list, page 3](04-destination-list-page-3.jpg) | Last page of station buttons |
| 5 | [Destination map](05-destination-map.jpg) | Line map with small station labels, scroll and zoom buttons |
| 6 | [Ticket type and quantity](06-ticket-type-and-quantity.jpg) | Single journey or group, − / + quantity (max 9), fare |
| 7 | [Ticket summary](07-ticket-summary.jpg) | Get in at / get down at, ticket type, quantity, fare, payment mode |
| 8 | [Scan to pay](08-scan-to-pay.jpg) | UPI QR with a countdown timer |

Every step relies on reading English text; station pictures, audio and colour cues
are absent.
